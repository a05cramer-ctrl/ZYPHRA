How a round works

One transaction an hour. That's all anyone sees.

  1. 01

    Fees

    100% of $ZYPHRA's pump.fun creator fees flow to the distributor wallet.

  2. 02

    Swap to ZEC

    On the hour, the SOL is swapped into native ZEC through NEAR Intents.

  3. 03

    One shielded tx

    One Orchard output per registered holder, pro rata to your balance at the snapshot.

  4. 04

    Your wallet

    ZEC lands in your shielded address. Only you can see it arrive.

Rules

      
What's true.

      
The full mechanic, including what isn't live yet.

    

    

      

        
          
Fee source100% of $ZYPHRA's pump.fun creator fees go to the distributor wallet, set at launch with pump.fun creator-fee sharing.

          
ScheduleEvery hour, at the top of the UTC hour.

          
SwapSOL into native ZEC through the NEAR Intents 1Click API, delivered to a Zcash address.

          
PayoutOne shielded Zcash transaction per round. One Orchard output per registered holder.

          
Your sharePro rata to your $ZYPHRA balance at the hourly snapshot.

          
Minimum hold500,000 $ZYPHRA.

          
Minimum payoutUnder 0.001 ZEC rolls over to the next hour.

          
AddressesUnified (u1) preferred. Sapling (zs1) accepted. Transparent (t1, t3) rejected.

          
What the chain showsOne shielded transaction an hour. Not who was paid, not how much.

          
What the distributor knowsThe wallet-to-address mapping it needs to pay you. It publishes only per-round totals and the round txid.

          
StatusDistributor and registry go live at launch. Anything marked Sample or Demonstration is illustrative.
